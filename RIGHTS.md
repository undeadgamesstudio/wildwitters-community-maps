# Rights for submitted maps (draft)

**Draft - not final and not legal advice.** The wording below is shown in the editor when you upload a map. It
will be reviewed before the first map is accepted; every submission stores the version of the text it was made under
(`rightsVersion` in `meta.json`).

Version: `draft-1`

When you upload a map to the Outwitters Community Hub you confirm:

> I made this map and every picture in it myself, or I have the right to use and pass on everything it contains.
> I give Undead Games a free, worldwide, permanent, non-exclusive right to use, copy, change, show and distribute the
> map and its pictures in Outwitters and for the Outwitters community, including in the public repository
> `outwitters-community-maps`. I stay named as the author. I can ask for my map to be removed; copies that were
> already made, and the history of the repository, may remain.

Not allowed in a submission:

- pictures, sounds or other material from the original game or from other people's work without the right to pass it on
- offensive, hateful or illegal content
- personal data of other people

What the system records with a submission: your account id, the time, the rights text version and a hash of the
submitted files.
